Match videos on social media

We record matches played in the game with our own desktop video tool and share them on Neon Football Simulator's YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. On TikTok, the tool uploads each video to our account's inbox as a draft, and we finish posting it in the TikTok app. The tool is used only by us and does not access anyone else's account.
